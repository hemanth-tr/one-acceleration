# EMI Calculator Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026  
**Developer:** Hemanth (GitHub: hemanth-tr)

## Your Calculations

EMI Calculator processes the principal, annual interest rate and loan tenure on your device to estimate monthly payments and a repayment schedule.

The app does not transmit these values to the developer or third parties. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases or developer-operated servers, and requests no Internet permission.

## Storage and Deletion

There is no saved loan history or database. Android may retain the current inputs and result visibility as temporary screen state to restore the screen after rotation or process recreation.

Clear the inputs to replace those values. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove app-managed local data.

The app disables Android backup and excludes app files from cloud backup and device transfer.

## Security and Sharing

Calculations run locally and app-managed data remains in Android's application sandbox. The developer does not receive, sell or share your calculation data.

Android, Google Play and your device's input method may process information under their own policies.

## Support

Privacy questions and support requests can be sent to:

[oneacceleration@gmail.com](mailto:oneacceleration@gmail.com)

Only include the information needed to describe your request. Do not send passwords or confidential loan details.

Your email provider handles email under its own policy. The developer uses information you voluntarily send to respond to your request and retains it only as long as needed for support.

The **Contact developer** button opens your email app and does not attach calculator inputs.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. Review the effective date for the current version.

## About the Calculator

Results are estimates for a fixed annual interest rate with monthly payments. Fees, taxes, insurance, rate changes and lender-specific rounding are excluded. Verify repayment terms with your lender.

The app does not provide loans, arrange lending, process payments or offer personalized financial advice.
